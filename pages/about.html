Our Story

Herbal knowledge, kept close to home.

AyurVeda Naturals started in a small kitchen where herbal preparations were made the way they'd been made for generations — by hand, in small batches, with ingredients you could name.

What began as gifts for family and friends grew into a formal practice: sourcing ingredients responsibly, testing for consistency, and packaging rituals that fit into modern daily life without losing what made them work in the first place.

Our Mission

Make traditional herbal wellness easy to live with, every day.

Not a lifestyle overhaul — small, honest rituals that fit into the routine you already have.

Ingredient Philosophy

Sourced with the same care we'd want for our own family.

We work with growers and cooperatives who share our approach to traditional cultivation, and every batch is tested before it's formulated into a finished product.

  • Direct relationships with growers where possible
  • Batch testing before formulation
  • Minimal, recyclable packaging
