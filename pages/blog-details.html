Building a Simple Morning Herbal Ritual

By Meera Nair · Sept 2, 2026 · 6 min read

On this page

  • 1. Start with warm water
  • 2. A five-minute tea ritual
  • 3. One mindful minute

Most morning-routine advice asks for more than most people can realistically give — a dozen steps before 7am, supplements timed to the minute. This isn't that. It's three small habits, each under a couple of minutes, that together make a noticeable difference over a few weeks.

1. Start with warm water

Before anything else — coffee included — a glass of warm water is a small, traditional habit worth keeping. It's simple enough that it doesn't need willpower to sustain.

Ingredient callout

Tulsi (holy basil) is often added to this first glass of water in Ayurvedic households — steep a few leaves the night before.

2. A five-minute tea ritual

Rather than treating tea as a caffeine delivery system, use it as a pause. Our Golden Turmeric Herbal Tea takes about five minutes to steep — long enough to genuinely wait rather than multitask.

3. One mindful minute

Before opening your phone, take one minute of quiet — even just finishing your tea in silence. It's a small anchor that makes the rest of the ritual stick.

This article reflects traditional practice and general wellness suggestions, not medical advice.
